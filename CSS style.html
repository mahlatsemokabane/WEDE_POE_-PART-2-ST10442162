Our story

Rooted in coffee, grown through community.

Brew & Bean Artisan Coffee was founded in 2018 by siblings Mia and James Peterson. They began with a small coffee cart at local farmers' markets, serving ethically sourced, single-origin coffee and homemade pastries.

In 2020, they opened their first brick-and-mortar location, which has become a neighbourhood gathering spot. Today, sustainability, ethical sourcing and a warm atmosphere remain at the heart of the business.

“To serve exceptional, ethically sourced coffee while creating a welcoming space where community connections are brewed daily.”

Our mission
